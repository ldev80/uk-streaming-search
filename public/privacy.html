Privacy Policy

What this site does

UK Freeview Search lets you search free UK streaming catalogues. The entire catalogue is downloaded to your browser and all searching happens locally on your device. No search queries are sent to any server.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies itself. Our hosting provider Cloudflare may set strictly necessary cookies for security and performance purposes (such as bot detection). These do not track you and do not require consent under GDPR.

Data collection

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data. There are no accounts, forms, analytics, or tracking scripts on this site.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Firebase (Google) and served through Cloudflare. No other third-party services are used. All fonts are self-hosted.
